Privacy Policy

Universal File Reader · Last updated: 27 October 2026

The short version: your files are opened and read on your own device. They are never uploaded, copied or seen by us or by anyone else. The app has no accounts. To keep it free, the Android app shows ads from Google AdMob; Google collects some information about your device to show and measure those ads (section 6).

1. Who we are

Universal File Reader is provided by Reaper ("we", "us"). You can contact us at kminnovations3@gmail.com.

2. Your files

  • When you choose or drop a file, it is opened and processed on your device, inside the app.
  • Your files are not uploaded to any server, and we do not receive, copy or store them.
  • Recent files: so the list is still there the next time you open the app, the app keeps a copy of each recently opened file (up to 12 files; files over 80 MB are not kept) in the app's own private storage on your device. These copies are never sent anywhere. Remove one with the × button, or all of them with "Clear list". Clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app also removes them.
  • Opening from other apps: if you choose Universal File Reader from another app's "Open with" or "Share" menu, the file is opened here. A temporary copy is held in the app's private storage and is deleted automatically after about a day (or kept in Recent files, as above).
  • Sharing, saving and printing: only when you tap Share, Save a copy or Print is a file handed to the app you choose (for example WhatsApp, Gmail or a printer) or saved in your Downloads folder. We do not see it, and what the other app does with it is covered by that app's own policy.
  • PDF tools, editing, scanning and reading text: merging, splitting, rearranging, shrinking, locking and unlocking PDFs, editing them, turning pictures or camera photos into a PDF, and reading the text of scanned pages (text recognition) all happen on your device, using software that is part of the app. They always make a new copy; the original file is never changed.
  • Folders you add: when you choose "Add a folder", Android's own folder picker gives the app access to that one folder, so it can list the documents inside and open the ones you tap. The app remembers which folders you added and which documents you starred as favourites. It does not copy the folder's contents anywhere, and you can remove a folder at any time in Settings.
  • Scan to PDF: the app asks your phone's camera app to take a picture. The app itself does not use the camera permission and never sees the camera; it only receives the picture you accepted.

3. What is saved on your device

The app saves a few things on your device. They stay on your device and are never sent to us or anyone else:

  • your settings (light or dark mode, how PDFs open, night mode for PDFs, text size and letters, reading speed, keep the screen on);
  • the list of folders you added and your favourites;
  • the copies of recent files described above;
  • where you stopped in each file (page, slide, place in a book, or playing time), so it can continue from there;
  • your bookmarks and the passages you highlighted in books;
  • if you add the home-screen widget: the name of the file you read last and where you stopped, shown on the widget (this stays on your device);
  • the words found inside your recent files, so "Search my files" is quick (a search list kept only on your device);
  • your signature drawing, only if you use the signature tool and choose to use it. Choose "Forget it" in the signature box, or clear the app's data, to delete it.

"Clear list" on the home screen deletes the saved copies, the remembered reading places and the search list. "Delete recent files, reading places and bookmarks" in Settings also deletes your bookmarks and highlights.

"Read aloud" uses the speech voice that is built into your phone. The app does not send the text anywhere; which voice is used, and whether it works offline, is decided by your phone's own text-to-speech settings.

4. Information we collect

We (Reaper) collect nothing. The app has no accounts, and we do not receive your name, email address, location, contacts, photos, files, device identifiers, usage statistics or crash reports. The ads in the Android app come from Google, which collects some information itself; see section 6.

5. Permissions

You choose each file yourself, using your device's normal file picker or another app's "Open with" and "Share" menus. The app does not ask for permission to browse all your files, and does not use your camera, microphone, contacts or location.

The Android app has internet access, and access to your device's advertising ID, only because Google's ad software needs them to load ads. The part of the app that opens your files is blocked from using the internet.

6. Ads and purchases

The Android app shows ads from Google AdMob: a banner at the bottom of the screen, and now and then a full-screen ad when you close a file. To show ads, limit how often you see them, measure them and prevent fraud, Google's ad software collects and sends to Google:

  • your device's advertising ID and other device identifiers;
  • your IP address, which gives a rough idea of where you are (country or city, not your exact location);
  • how you interact with the ads (for example, whether an ad was shown or tapped);
  • technical information about the app and your device, and crash reports from the ad software.

The ad software never receives your files, their names or their contents. If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before Google may use your data for personalised ads. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ads → Privacy choices for ads. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads, or Google → Ads, depending on the phone).

How Google uses this information is explained at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and in Google's privacy policy (policies.google.com/privacy).

The app offers no purchases yet. If a paid "Premium" option (no ads) is added, payments will be handled by Google Play; we will never see your card details.

7. Children

The app is a general-purpose tool and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

8. Your rights

Because we hold no personal information about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. Information collected by Google for ads is handled under Google's privacy policy, which explains how to see and control it. If you have a question about privacy, write to kminnovations3@gmail.com.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles information, we will update this page and the date at the top before the change takes effect.
