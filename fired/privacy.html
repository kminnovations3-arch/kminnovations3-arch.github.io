Privacy Policy

FIRED! · Last updated: 24 November 2026

The short version: FIRED! has no accounts and we do not collect your name, email, contacts, photos or location. Your best score and settings are saved on your own phone. The game shows ads from Google AdMob, and Google collects some information about your device to show and measure those ads (section 4). The game has no purchases.

1. Who we are

FIRED! is a game made by KM Innovations ("we", "us"). You can contact us at firedgame.support@gmail.com.

2. What is saved on your device

  • Your best score, the ways to die you have found (the Death Album), the day you last saw the daily comic, and your settings (sound and music) are saved in the game's own private storage on your phone.
  • This saved game is not sent to us or to anyone else.
  • Clearing the game's data in your phone's settings, or uninstalling the game, deletes it.

3. Information we collect

We do not collect, receive or store any personal information about you. The game has no sign-up, no login and no chat. If you email us, we receive your email address and what you write, and use them only to reply.

4. Ads

To keep the game free, it shows ads from Google AdMob: a short full-screen advert now and then before you play again, and a video you can choose to watch for a spare umbrella. To show these ads, limit how often you see them, measure them and prevent fraud, Google's software in the game collects information such as:

  • your phone's advertising ID;
  • your IP address, and the rough location that can be worked out from it;
  • details of your phone (model, operating system, language, screen size);
  • how you interact with the ads you see, and diagnostic information if the ads software crashes.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent the first time you open it, and ads are only personalised if you agree. You can change your choice at any time with Privacy choices at the bottom of the game's home screen. Elsewhere, you can turn off personalised ads in your phone's settings (Google → Ads), where you can also reset or delete your advertising ID.

How Google uses this information is described in Google's own policies: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

5. Age

FIRED! is a dark comedy meant for players aged 16 and over. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, email us and we will delete it.

6. Your rights

Because we do not hold personal information about you, there is normally nothing for us to show, correct or delete; your saved game is on your phone and deleting the game's data removes it. For information Google collects for ads, you can use the choices in section 4 and Google's own tools at My Ad Center. For any question about your privacy, email us at firedgame.support@gmail.com.

7. Changes to this policy

If the game changes in a way that affects your privacy (for example if we add purchases or an optional sign-in for leaderboards), we will update this page and the date at the top before the change reaches the game.
