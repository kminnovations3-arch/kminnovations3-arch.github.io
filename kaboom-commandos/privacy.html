Privacy Policy

Kaboom Commandos · Last updated: 11 November 2026

The short version: Kaboom Commandos has no accounts and we do not collect your name, email, contacts, photos or location. Your progress is saved on your own phone. The game shows ads from Google AdMob, and Google collects some information about your device to show and measure those ads (section 4). Purchases are handled by Google Play; we never see your card details (section 5).

1. Who we are

Kaboom Commandos is a game made by KM Innovations ("we", "us"). You can contact us at kaboomcommandos.help@gmail.com.

2. What is saved on your device

  • Your game progress (stages, stars, coins, squad upgrades, vehicles and Legends you own, medals, the daily challenge and event results) and your settings (sound, vibration, controls) are saved in the game's own private storage on your phone.
  • This saved game is not sent to us or to anyone else.
  • Clearing the game's data in your phone's settings, or uninstalling the game, deletes it.

3. Information we collect

We do not collect, receive or store any personal information about you. The game has no sign-up, no login and no chat. If you email us, we receive your email address and what you write, and use them only to reply.

4. Ads

To keep the game free, it shows ads from Google AdMob: a full-screen advert now and then between stages, and adverts you can choose to watch for a reward. To show these ads, limit how often you see them, measure them and prevent fraud, Google's software in the game collects information such as:

  • your phone's advertising ID;
  • your IP address, and the rough location that can be worked out from it;
  • details of your phone (model, operating system, language, screen size);
  • how you interact with the ads you see, and diagnostic information if the ads software crashes.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent the first time you open it, and ads are only personalised if you agree. You can change your choice at any time under Settings → Privacy choices in the game. Elsewhere, you can turn off personalised ads in your phone's settings (Google → Ads), where you can also reset or delete your advertising ID.

How Google uses this information is described in Google's own policies: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Buying Remove Ads in the game stops the adverts shown between stages.

5. Purchases

You can buy items in the game (for example Legends, vehicles, Remove Ads or a Brigade Pass). Every purchase is made through Google Play. Google handles your payment; we never see or store your card or payment details. The game only receives a confirmation from Google that an item was bought, so it can give it to you. Google Play's terms and privacy policy apply to the payment.

6. Children

Kaboom Commandos is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, email us and we will delete it.

7. Your rights

Because we do not hold personal information about you, there is normally nothing for us to show, correct or delete; your saved game is on your phone and deleting the game's data removes it. For information Google collects for ads, you can use the choices in section 4 and Google's own tools at My Ad Center. For any question about your privacy, email us at kaboomcommandos.help@gmail.com.

8. Changes to this policy

If the game changes in a way that affects your privacy (for example if we add an optional Google Play Games sign-in for cloud saves or leaderboards), we will update this page and the date at the top before the change reaches the game.
